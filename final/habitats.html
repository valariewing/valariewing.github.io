Alien Habitat Comparison

This page compares the natural and preferred habitats of the Predator, Xenomorph, and Harvester species.

Predator Habitat

Yautja often hunt in dangerous environments such as jungles, forests, war zones, and remote planets. They prefer places where prey is difficult to track and where survival requires strength and skill. They live in a rather arid planet with low oxygen and extreme heat called Yautja Prime.

Xenomorph Habitat

Xenomorphs can survive in dark, enclosed, and hostile environments. They are commonly found in spaceships, underground hives, abandoned colonies, and isolated planets where they can build and protect their hive. Since they are invasive, they don't really have a home planet they come from, as they were engineered.

Harvester Habitat

Harvesters thrive in large, open, and devastated environments. They are often connected to destroyed ecosystems, alien battlefields, and planets where they consume resources and dominate the land.

Similarities and Differences

Similarities

  • All three species can survive in dangerous environments.
  • All three are connected to violence, survival, and domination.
  • Each species adapts to its surroundings differently.
  • All three are threats to humans and other life forms.

Differences

  • Yautja choose habitats for hunting.
  • Xenomorphs choose habitats for nesting and reproduction.
  • Harvesters destroy habitats by consuming resources.
  • Yautja rely on technology and strength, Xenomorphs rely on biology, and Harvesters rely on size and force.
