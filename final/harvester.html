Species Overview

Harvesters are enormous alien creatures known for consuming entire ecosystems and stripping planets of resources. They are heavily armored and extremely destructive, making them one of the most dangerous alien species in the galaxy.

Traits

Harvesters possess overwhelming brute force capable of destroying structures and vehicles with ease.
Their thick exoskeleton armor protects weapons and harsh planetary environments.
Despite their size, Harvesters demonstrate tactical instincts and coordinated behavior during attacks.
