Alien Life Cycles

This page compares how Predators, Xenomorphs, and Harvesters grow, reproduce, and survive through different stages of life.

Predator Life Cycle

Predators begin as young members of a warrior society. As they grow, they train in combat, hunting, and survival. To become respected adults, they must prove themselves through dangerous hunts and earn trophies.

Xenomorph Life Cycle

Xenomorphs have one of the most dangerous life cycles. They begin as eggs, hatch into facehuggers, infect a host, grow into chestbursters, and eventually become adult Xenomorphs such as drones, warriors, or queens.

Harvester Life Cycle

Harvesters grow into massive destructive organisms built for survival and domination. Their life cycle focuses on gaining size, strength, armor, and the ability to consume resources from planets or ecosystems.

Similarities and Differences

Similarities

  • All three species become more dangerous as they mature.
  • Each species has a life cycle connected to survival.
  • All three grow into powerful threats.
  • Their adult forms are much stronger than their early forms.

Differences

  • Predators grow through training and culture.
  • Xenomorphs grow through parasitic reproduction.
  • Harvesters grow through size, armor, and consumption.
  • Predators rely on skill, Xenomorphs rely on biology, and Harvesters rely on destruction.
