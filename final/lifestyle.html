Alien Lifestyle Comparison

This page compares the lifestyle, behavior, social structure, and survival habits of the Predator, Xenomorph, and Harvester species.

Predator Lifestyle

Predators live by a warrior and hunter lifestyle. Their culture values strength, honor, skill, and trophies earned from dangerous hunts. They travel across planets searching for worthy opponents.

Xenomorph Lifestyle

Xenomorphs live as part of a hive system controlled by a Queen. Their lifestyle focuses on survival, reproduction, protecting the hive, and spreading their species through hosts.

Harvester Lifestyle

Harvesters live through destruction and consumption. Their behavior focuses on dominating environments, consuming resources, and overwhelming anything that threatens them.

Similarities and Differences

Similarities

  • All three species are dangerous and aggressive.
  • All three focus on survival in hostile environments.
  • Each species uses its strengths to dominate others.
  • All three are major threats to humans and other life forms.

Differences

  • Yautja live by honor and hunting traditions.
  • Xenomorphs live through hive instincts and reproduction.
  • Harvesters live through destruction and consumption.
  • Yautja use technology, Xenomorphs use biology, and Harvesters use size and power.
