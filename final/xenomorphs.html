Xenomorph Species

Species Overview

Xenomorphs are aggressive extraterrestrial creatures known for their deadly biology and hive behavior. They reproduce through parasitic implantation and rapidly evolve depending on their host organism. Xenomorphs are feared for their speed, intelligence, and acid blood.

Xenomorph Types

The Drone is the standard Xenomorph type responsible for scouting, capturing hosts, and defending the hive.
Warriors are stronger, combat-focused Xenomorphs that protect the Queen and attack threats aggressively.
The Queen is the massive leader of the hive responsible for laying eggs and controlling the colony while being highly intelligent.
